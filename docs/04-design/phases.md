# Phase Design Spec

This document defines the experience of each phase from the user's perspective: what they see, what agents are doing, what success looks like, and how the transition between phases is handled.

---

## Overview

| Phase | Days | Theme | User's emotional job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orient & Observe | 1–30 | Understand | Feel less lost |
| Experiment & Explore | 31–60 | Discover | Feel curious, not anxious |
| Decide & Position | 61–90 | Act | Feel prepared, not panicked |

Phases are based on the user's **role start date**, not app install date. If a user installs on day 14, they begin in Phase 1 with 16 days remaining.

---

## Phase 1 — Orient & Observe (Days 1–30)

### User's emotional job
The user is likely overwhelmed, unsure of unwritten rules, and anxious about being judged. The app's job in this phase is to make them feel less alone and help them make sense of what is happening around them — not to optimise their performance.

### Goals (auto-generated by PathArchitect)

| Goal | Type | Due day |
|---|---|---|
| Complete a team map (who does what, who to go to for what) | Relationship | Day 7 |
| Have your first 1:1 with your manager | Relationship | Day 5 |
| Identify 3 tasks that feel energising | Self-awareness | Day 14 |
| Identify 2 tasks that feel draining | Self-awareness | Day 14 |
| Attend or observe one cross-functional meeting | Observation | Day 21 |
| Complete your first independent piece of work | Delivery | Day 28 |

Goals are adapted by PathArchitect based on role archetype. A data analyst's goals will reference specific data tasks; a marketing associate's will reference content and campaigns.

### Success signals
- At least 7 check-ins completed
- At least 4 goals marked complete
- At least 3 energy tags collected (for SignalScout baseline)
- Manager 1:1 completed

### Agent activity in this phase

| Agent | Activity |
|---|---|
| PathArchitect | Initial plan generation on day 1; first weekly review on day 7 |
| ExperienceCoach | Evening check-ins every day (days 1–14), then 3x/week |
| CommsCoach | On-demand only in phase 1 (no proactive scripts yet) |
| SignalScout | Baseline tracking; begins collecting energy tags; no fit scores surfaced yet |

### What the user sees
- **Home screen:** Phase 1 banner with days remaining, today's top task, check-in prompt
- **Plan screen:** 30-day view with goals grouped by week, phase progress bar
- **Check-in screen:** Confidence score, energy score, free text, tag picker
- **Suggestions inbox:** PathArchitect plan proposals, ExperienceCoach micro-actions
- **No fit scores yet** — SignalScout is building a baseline; showing scores before day 30 would be misleading

### Phase 1 → 2 transition
- Automatic at day 30 (or on the Monday closest to day 30)
- PathArchitect generates Phase 2 goals and presents them as a proposal
- User sees a "Phase complete" moment: brief summary card ("Here's what you learned in your first 30 days")
- Card shows: goals completed, check-ins done, top 2 energy tags, first micro-signal about fit direction

## Phase 2 — Experiment & Explore (Days 31–60)

### User's emotional job
The user now has enough of a map to start taking low-risk experiments. Anxiety shifts from "I don't know what's going on" to "I don't know what I want or what I'm good at." The app's job is to make exploration feel safe, structured, and genuinely interesting.

### Goals (auto-generated by PathArchitect)

| Goal | Type | Due day |
|---|---|---|
| Take on one stretch task slightly outside your comfort zone | Growth | Day 40 |
| Shadow someone in a role that interests you | Exploration | Day 45 |
| Have one exploratory coffee chat with someone in a target role | Exploration | Day 50 |
| Complete a second independent piece of work (increased scope) | Delivery | Day 55 |
| Attend a meeting in a different team or function | Observation | Day 50 |
| Write a short reflection: what surprised you so far? | Self-awareness | Day 60 |

### Success signals
- At least 2 exploration activities completed (shadowing, coffee chats)
- At least 10 total check-ins since app start
- At least one archetype has a fit score above 0.30 ("Weak" or above)
- Day-45 fit-check reflection completed

### Agent activity in this phase

| Agent | Activity |
|---|---|
| PathArchitect | Weekly review continues; introduces exploration tasks |
| ExperienceCoach | 3x/week check-ins; increasingly asks about exploration experiences |
| CommsCoach | Proactive: generates coffee chat and shadowing request scripts 48h before tasks are due |
| SignalScout | Full fit-score updates active; day-45 fit-check triggered; weekly digest begins |

### What the user sees
- **Home screen:** Phase 2 banner, exploration activity cards, fit-score teaser ("Your signals are building...")
- **Explore screen (new in phase 2):** Role archetype cards showing current signal strength; tappable to see details and suggested exploration tasks for that archetype
- **Fit-check card (day 45):** Ranked fit signals with plain-language labels, reflection prompt ("What surprised you? What confirmed what you already suspected?")
- **CommsCoach:** Pre-generated scripts appear in Suggestions inbox before exploration tasks

### Phase 2 → 3 transition
- Automatic at day 60
- PathArchitect generates Phase 3 goals focused on reflection and positioning
- "Phase complete" moment: updated fit scores, top archetype highlighted, manager conversation teaser

## Phase 3 — Decide & Position (Days 61–90)

### User's emotional job
The user has 30+ days of evidence. The job now is to make sense of it and act on it — specifically, to have a productive development conversation with their manager and leave the 90 days with a clear direction. The risk is paralysis or vagueness. The app's job is to make the decision feel concrete and the conversation feel achievable.

### Goals (auto-generated by PathArchitect)

| Goal | Type | Due day |
|---|---|---|
| Review your fit scores and write a 3-sentence reflection | Self-awareness | Day 65 |
| Identify your preferred development direction | Decision | Day 70 |
| Prepare your manager development conversation using CommsCoach | Comms | Day 75 |
| Have the manager development conversation | Delivery | Day 80 |
| Complete the day-90 fit-check reflection | Reflection | Day 90 |
| Export your growth log | Output | Day 90 |

### Success signals
- Manager development conversation task marked complete
- Day-90 fit-check reflection completed
- Growth log exported or shared
- At least one archetype with fit score above 0.40 ("Emerging" or above)

### Agent activity in this phase

| Agent | Activity |
|---|---|
| PathArchitect | Generates post-90-day continuation plan proposal at day 85 |
| ExperienceCoach | 3x/week check-ins; shift in tone toward reflection and celebration |
| CommsCoach | Proactive: generates full manager development conversation script at day 72 |
| SignalScout | Day-90 fit-check triggered; final fit-score calculation; weekly digest continues |

### What the user sees
- **Home screen:** Countdown to day 90, manager conversation task prominent
- **Fit-score screen (full view):** All archetypes ranked, evidence summary ("You rated data tasks as energising 11 times"), reflection prompts
- **Growth log screen:** Auto-generated summary of the 90 days — goals completed, confidence trend, top fit signal, key moments. Exportable as PDF or shareable link.
- **Post-90 plan proposal:** PathArchitect suggests what to focus on in the next 30 days. User can accept, modify, or pause the app.

---

## Cross-Phase Rules

- Users cannot manually skip to a later phase. Phases advance automatically by day.
- Users can always view upcoming phase goals in read-only mode.
- If a user falls significantly behind on goals, SignalScout triggers PathArchitect to simplify the plan — goals are never automatically marked complete.
- A user who starts the app late (e.g. day 20 of their role) skips completed phases and begins from the appropriate point in their current phase.
- Phase transitions always include a "moment" — a screen or card that acknowledges progress. No phase ends silently.
