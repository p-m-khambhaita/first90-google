# Competitive Landscape

> Analysis of direct and indirect competitors to First90, and where First90 is differentiated.

---

## Competitive Map

Competitors fall into four categories:

1. **AI career coaching tools** — broad career development, not role-specific
2. **Onboarding platforms** — employer-side tools, not user-owned
3. **Static 30-60-90 templates** — no AI, no adaptation
4. **Human coaching / mentoring services** — high quality but high cost and inaccessible

---

## Direct Competitors

### Path AI
- **Focus:** General career growth, goal tracking, habit formation
- **Audience:** Mid-career professionals
- **AI depth:** Goal-setting assistant, some reflection prompts
- **Gap vs First90:** No 90-day time-box, no internal role-fit discovery, no agentic plan adaptation, no comms coaching
- **Pricing:** ~$10–15/month

### Rocky.ai
- **Focus:** Professional development coaching, team performance
- **Audience:** Broad — individuals and teams
- **AI depth:** Chat-based coaching, structured reflection
- **Gap vs First90:** Not early-career specific, no role-fit discovery, no phase structure, no proactive agent layer
- **Pricing:** ~$12/month individual; enterprise pricing for teams

### Career Compass AI
- **Focus:** CV optimisation, interview prep, job search
- **Audience:** Job seekers and career changers
- **AI depth:** CV rewriting, cover letter generation, job match scoring
- **Gap vs First90:** Entirely external/job-search focused — stops at the offer. First90 starts where Career Compass ends.
- **Pricing:** Freemium, ~$10–20/month paid

### Himalayas AI Coach
- **Focus:** Career path discovery, skills mapping
- **Audience:** Early-to-mid career
- **AI depth:** Chat-based career exploration, role recommendations
- **Gap vs First90:** No daily check-ins, no agentic plan, no comms coaching, no time-boxed structure, external focus
- **Pricing:** Free beta (as of 2025)

### Google Career Dreamer
- **Focus:** Career exploration, skills-to-roles mapping
- **Audience:** Broad, students and early career
- **AI depth:** Skills inventory, role suggestions, career path visualisation
- **Gap vs First90:** No daily support, no plan, no comms coaching, no role-fit scoring within a company — purely external exploration
- **Pricing:** Free

## Indirect Competitors

### Employer onboarding platforms (Enboarder, Leapsome, Workday Learning)
- **Who buys it:** HR / People teams, not individual users
- **What they do:** Structured onboarding workflows, check-in scheduling, learning content
- **Gap vs First90:** Company-owned (not user-owned), generic content, no AI adaptation, no role-fit discovery, abandoned after week 2 in most deployments
- **Why they matter:** If First90 moves into B2B, these are the incumbent tools it would sit alongside or replace

### BetterUp
- **Focus:** Professional coaching, leadership development
- **Audience:** Mid-to-senior professionals (employer-sponsored)
- **AI depth:** AI-augmented human coaching sessions
- **Gap vs First90:** High cost (~$300–500/month employer-sponsored), not accessible to early-career workers, not time-boxed, not focused on role fit
- **Why they matter:** Sets the "premium" benchmark for professional coaching — First90 undercuts this by orders of magnitude

### Static 30-60-90 day plan templates (Notion, Google Docs, HBR articles)
- **What they are:** Free templates shared online, often downloaded once and abandoned
- **Gap vs First90:** No AI, no adaptation, no check-ins, no role-fit — they're documents, not tools
- **Why they matter:** Many early-career workers start here. First90 should position itself as "the version of this that actually works"

### Human mentors and coaches
- **What they are:** Formal or informal mentorship, paid career coaches
- **Gap vs First90:** Human coaches cost £50–150/hr and require scheduling; most early-career workers don't have access to a formal mentor at all
- **Why they matter:** The gold standard in quality — First90 doesn't replace this but makes the outcome accessible to people who can't get the real thing

## First90's Defensible Differentiators

1. **Scope specificity** — the only tool built exclusively for the first 90 days in a role already held. Not job search, not whole career.
2. **Internal role-fit discovery** — no competitor surfaces "you might fit better in the data team" based on your own energy signals inside your current company.
3. **Agentic adaptation** — the plan reacts to what's actually happening, not what was planned on day one.
4. **Privacy-first architecture** — sensitive reflections stay on-device. No competitor makes this promise.
5. **Price point** — £8–15/month or a one-time £25–40 bundle is accessible to graduates and apprentices on starting salaries.

## Competitive Risk

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Large player (LinkedIn, Google) adds a "first 90 days" feature | Medium | Move fast, build user loyalty, focus on depth over breadth |
| Employer onboarding platform adds AI coaching layer | Medium | First90 is user-owned — this is a structural advantage that employer tools can't replicate |
| Rocky.ai or Path AI narrows scope to early-career | Low | First90 has the role-fit + privacy architecture as a moat; scope alone isn't enough |
| Free templates + ChatGPT replaces the need | Low | Users need structure, accountability, and adaptation — not a blank prompt |
