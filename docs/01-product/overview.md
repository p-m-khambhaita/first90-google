# Product Overview — First90

## Mission

> Help early professionals survive, build confidence, and discover where they truly fit inside their company in their first 90 days.

First90 is not a generic career coach. It is tightly scoped to the most universally painful moment in an early career: the first three months in a new role, where you're expected to perform but given very little structured support to do so.

---

## Problem Statement

Early-career workers — graduates, apprentices, new hires — face a consistent set of problems in their first 90 days:

1. **No mentor or structured support.** Most companies intend to provide mentorship but rarely follow through. Managers are busy. Buddies are informal. Early hires are largely left to figure things out.

2. **Low confidence in communication.** Writing to a senior stakeholder, asking for more responsibility, raising a mistake — all feel disproportionately high-stakes when you're new.

3. **Unclear expectations.** "What does good look like here?" is rarely answered explicitly. New hires spend weeks inferring unwritten rules.

4. **Wrong role, no visibility.** Many graduates and apprentices are placed into roles that don't match their natural strengths. They don't know what other internal roles exist or how to explore them without appearing disloyal.

5. **Generic onboarding resources.** Static 30-60-90 day templates, induction documents, and onboarding portals are forgotten within days. They don't adapt to what's actually happening.

The result: early-career workers either over-index on performance anxiety, disengage quietly, or leave — often within 12–18 months — because they couldn't find their footing.

---

## Target User

### Primary
- Graduates in their first corporate role (22–24)
- Degree apprentices or school leavers entering a structured programme (18–22)
- New hires in their first 1–3 professional roles

### Secondary
- Anyone starting a new role internally: lateral move, promotion, department transfer

### Not for
- Senior hires (5+ years experience)
- Career changers looking externally
- Job seekers and people between roles

### User characteristics
- Tech-comfortable, mobile-first
- Motivated but anxious — they want to do well but don't know what "well" looks like
- Likely have no or poor access to mentorship at work
- Willing to self-reflect if the tool feels safe and non-judgmental

## The Three Phases

First90 replaces the generic "Learn → Contribute → Lead" model with a more honest, early-career-appropriate structure:

### Phase 1: Orient & Observe (Days 1–30)
**Theme:** Understand before acting.

The first month is about psychological safety and information gathering. The user should not be optimising for performance yet — they should be mapping the terrain.

- Learn the team structure, decision-making style, and communication norms
- Identify which tasks feel energising vs draining
- Start building a strengths profile through structured self-reflection
- Complete 2–3 relationship-building goals (meet key colleagues, have first 1:1)
- Agent activity: PathArchitect sets the initial plan; ExperienceCoach runs nightly check-ins; SignalScout begins baseline tracking

### Phase 2: Experiment & Explore (Days 31–60)
**Theme:** Test and discover.

With a basic map of the terrain, the user begins low-risk experiments. The focus shifts from "survive" to "discover."

- Take on 1–2 stretch tasks slightly outside the comfort zone
- Shadow someone in a different internal role
- Have 1–2 exploratory coffee chats with people in roles that match emerging interests
- Begin comparing energy markers against role archetypes
- Agent activity: PathArchitect introduces exploration tasks into the plan; SignalScout begins updating fit scores; a fit-check reflection is triggered at day 45

### Phase 3: Decide & Position (Days 61–90)
**Theme:** Act and prepare.

With 60 days of evidence, the user can now make informed decisions about their direction and have a productive development conversation with their manager.

- Review fit scores across archetypes and reflect on findings
- Identify a preferred development direction (deepen current role OR explore internal move)
- Prepare and deliver a manager development conversation
- Create a rough 90-day-forward plan
- Agent activity: CommsCoach generates the manager conversation script; SignalScout triggers a final fit-check; PathArchitect proposes a post-90-day continuation plan

## Why This Is Different

### vs. Generic career coaches (Rocky.ai, BetterUp, Path AI)
Those tools are designed for mid-career professionals with broad goals. First90 is time-boxed to 90 days and role-specific. It's a focused tool for a focused problem.

### vs. Job search / CV tools (Himalayas, Career Compass)
First90 is entirely post-hire. It helps you succeed *in* the role you've got, not find a new one.

### vs. Static 30-60-90 templates
A static template doesn't know you skipped three check-ins last week or that you've been flagging client calls as draining. First90 adapts based on real behaviour.

### vs. Company onboarding portals
Those are company-owned, company-controlled, and often abandoned after week one. First90 is user-owned. It works for the person, not the employer.

## Core Features (v1)

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Role setup | User inputs company, title, start date; PathArchitect generates initial 30-60-90 plan |
| Daily check-in | Confidence score (1–10), energy score, free text, tags — runs each evening |
| Plan view | Visual 30-60-90 day plan with phase, goals, and status |
| Micro-actions | 1–3 suggested tomorrow actions from ExperienceCoach after each check-in |
| CommsCoach | Paste a draft message → receive 2–3 variants with explanations |
| Role-fit tracker | Emerging fit scores across archetypes, surfaced progressively |
| Suggestions inbox | All agent proposals in one place — accept, modify, or dismiss |

## Monetisation Hypothesis

### B2C (initial go-to-market)

**Free tier:**
- Role setup and basic 30-60-90 plan
- Daily check-ins (unlimited)
- Confidence and energy tracking
- Basic CommsCoach (1 variant per message)

**Paid tier — ~£8–15/month or ~£25–40 one-time 90-day bundle:**
- Role-fit analysis and archetype scoring
- Exploration tracks (coffee chat scripts, shadowing tasks, fit-check reflections)
- Advanced CommsCoach (3 variants, proactive scripts, tone modes)
- Manager conversation preparation
- Exportable growth log (shareable summary of your 90 days)

**Rationale:** The free tier is genuinely useful. The paid tier unlocks the most valuable and differentiated features — role-fit and proactive comms — that users will hit the ceiling on quickly.

### B2B (later)

Sell to employers, apprenticeship providers, and graduate scheme operators as a lightweight early-career experience platform:
- Per-seat annual pricing
- Cohort insights dashboard (anonymised) for HR/People teams
- White-labelled onboarding variant (First90 for `[Company]`)
- Key pitch: "Increase early-talent retention by helping new hires find the right internal role before they leave to find it elsewhere."
